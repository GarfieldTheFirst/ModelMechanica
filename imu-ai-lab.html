Threshold (σ) — how far from normal triggers an alert
1 – 2σ Sensitive. Flags ~5–30 % of samples under Gaussian noise. Use when subtle deviations matter: gait analysis, early-warning vibration shifts, activity recognition.
2 – 3σ Balanced. ~0.3–5 % false-positive rate. Good general purpose starting point for most monitoring tasks.
3 – 5σ Conservative. Only extreme outliers (<0.3 % of normal samples). Use for safety-critical alerts: impact detection, mechanical fault alarms, structural damage.
Window (samples) — baseline memory at ~50 Hz
20 – 50 ~0.4–1 s. Very responsive baseline. Catches sharp transient events but adapts quickly — normal activity after a spike resets baseline fast. Use for: taps, drops, impacts.
50 – 200 ~1–4 s. Balanced. Baseline stable enough to distinguish activity changes (walking vs. running) without being fooled by brief spikes.
200 – 500 ~4–10 s. Very stable baseline. Best for slow state changes: machine idle vs. loaded, structural resonance shifts, long-term vibration drift. Slow to adapt after a regime change.
